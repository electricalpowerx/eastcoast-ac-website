Frequently Asked Questions

Should I use a torch to thaw pipes?
No. Open flame is a fire and carbon monoxide risk. Restore heat and use safe methods only.
Do you repair burst pipes?
Burst pipe repair is plumbing. We restore the heating system that caused the freeze and will be clear when you need a plumber.
Can space heaters replace a furnace overnight?
Only as a short, carefully watched stopgap. They are not a substitute for a working heating system.

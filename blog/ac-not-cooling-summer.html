AC Not Cooling: What Usually Failed (and What Can Wait)

East Coast AC & Heating • 5 min read

An outdoor fan spinning does not mean you have cooling. Lower Mainland heat waves expose neglected condensers, failed capacitors, and undersized systems.

Safe things to check

Thermostat in cool, set below room temperature. Filter not clogged. Outdoor unit free of debris and standing water. Breaker not tripped.
